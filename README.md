### [1. Introduction](./1_Introduction.pdf)
Introduces local (forbidden-position) and global (cycle-structure) permutation restrictions, summarizes the main theorems, and notes historical attribution.

### [2. Derangements](./2_Derangements.pdf)
Derives the closed-form formula and recurrences for derangement numbers $D_n$, and proves the sharp two-sided error bound $\frac{1}{n+2} < \left|D_n - \frac{n!}{e}\right| < \frac{1}{n+1}$.

### [3. Forbidden Positions and Rook Polynomials](./3_ForbiddenPositionsandRookPolynomials.pdf)
Generalizes derangements to arbitrary forbidden boards, proving the rook-polynomial counting theorem alongside its product and deletion–contraction rules.

### [4. Cycle Structure](./4_CycleStructure.pdf)
Develops the unsigned Stirling numbers of the first kind and their generating function, then uses cycle insertion to give a combinatorial proof of the derangement recurrence.

### [5. Main Investigation: The Staircase Board](./5_TheStaircaseBoard.pdf)
Finds the rook numbers and closed formula for the staircase board ($N_n$), proves the ménage relation $U_n = N_n - N_{n-1}$ both algebraically and bijectively, and establishes the asymptotic limit $N_n / n! \to e^{-2}$.
